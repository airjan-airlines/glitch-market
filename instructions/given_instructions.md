B@B Technical Take-Home: 

Build a marketplace where autonomous agents buy and sell information on-chain that the buyer cannot inspect before paying.

Choose a specific vertical, such as cybersecurity intelligence, scientific research, model evaluations, sports scouting, governance analysis, or another domain you find compelling. The market must be designed around that vertical’s actual participants, evidence, incentives, and failure modes. A generic marketplace with themed labels is not sufficient.

You decide how information is delivered, how sellers establish credibility, how buyers receive protection, and how payment, verification, disputes, and reputation work.

Your solution should interact with a public testnet ON CHAIN. You may use any stack, tools, or AI systems (and we heavily encourage AI). Be agentic, read the docs, and be resourceful. We care about your product judgment, the credibility of your mechanism, and the quality of the finished experience, not how much code you write or even the code itself.

This project is intentionally very unstructured. We’re looking for how close to deployment you can take this in a short period of time and how useful your project actually is. Please don’t spend too much time on this and make sure to get your 8 hours of sleep and get your homework in first. A passing project could be completed in 1-2 hours.
